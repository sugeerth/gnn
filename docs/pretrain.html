Cora citation network · self-supervised GNNs

Seven labels.
2,708 papers.

What can a graph neural network learn about seven research areas when it gets one labelled paper per area? This lab pre-trains five label-free objectives on the citation graph, fine-tunes them with 1 to 20 labels per class, and then stacks post-training on top. Hyperparameters are chosen on the validation nodes, and the test nodes are only scored.

Stage 1 · pre-training

What the encoder learns before it sees a label

Each encoder is frozen and scored two ways: a logistic-regression probe on the 140 public training labels, and k-means against the true areas (NMI). The grey reference is the same GCN with random weights, a strong baseline on Cora because propagation alone already smooths features over neighbours.
